# Data Interchange in UE5 (FBX, OpenUSD, GLB)

Slides from the live Unreal Engine class **Data Interchange in UE5 (FBX, OpenUSD, GLB)**, taught 2026-09-30 by Alex Coulombe Presents. One scene goes out of Unreal through OpenUSD and GLB, into Godot and Three.js, and back in again. Along the way it measures what each format keeps and what it drops.

**View the deck:** https://ibrews.github.io/data-interchange-ue5-class/

Built on [Spatial Deck](https://github.com/ibrews/spatial-deck), a single-file HTML presentation framework.

## Quickstart

Open the link above, or clone this repo and open `index.html` in any browser. No build step.

## Things to Try

1. Press `→` or `Space` to step through the slides; press `G` for the slide grid.
2. Jump to slide 6, "The pipeline, and what falls off", and watch the animated diagram of what each export hop drops.
3. Open "Same scene: stock vs our bridges" and compare the four export routes cell by cell.
4. Press `N` to open the presenter window with the speaker notes.
5. Append `?vertical` to the URL to read the whole deck as one scrolling page.
